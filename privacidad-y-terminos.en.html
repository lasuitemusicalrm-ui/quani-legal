Offline-First Architecture

1. Privacy Policy: Your Finances Live Only on Your iPhone

Quani was built on one strict principle: your wealth, your accounts and your financial habits belong to you alone. Quani has no servers of its own, requires no email sign-up, and contains no advertising trackers.

Quani is designed to work offline: the accounting engine, the advisor, receipt reading and every budget calculation run entirely inside the device. Even so, the app can make one network connection, always under your control and described in detail in section 3. It does not transmit information about your accounts, your transactions or your budgets.

Per-Profile SQLite Database

Your accounts (debit, credit, assets and liabilities), transactions filtered by date range, accumulated balances, budgets, folders, shortcuts and photographs are stored locally on your device.

Keychain Security

Each profile's PIN is kept encrypted in the native iOS Keychain and is never included in plain text inside exported backups.

No Analytics, No Ad Profiles

Quani ships no analytics SDK, builds no advertising profile and does not measure how you use the app.

Private Financial Intelligence

2. Deterministic Engine (Advisor) and On-Device Intelligence (AdvisorAI)

Every question, liquidity diagnosis, year-over-year comparison, savings plan and receipt reading is processed inside your phone without sending your statements to external services:

2.1 Built-in Deterministic Engine (Advisor)

This is Quani's main engine and it works on every iPhone, including the oldest ones. It calculates your real balances to the cent, the debt accumulated after each transaction, calendar projections and financial priorities directly on your local database. No generative model can alter or invent accounting figures.

2.2 AdvisorAI Depending on Your iPhone Generation

AdvisorAI is the optional conversation layer, and how it works depends on the phone you own:

  • iPhone 15 Pro and later, with iOS 26 or later and Apple Intelligence enabled: AdvisorAI talks to the model built into iOS, with no additional downloads (0 MB). The model runs on your device and works with your own figures and your profile context.
  • On every other iPhone, or with Apple Intelligence turned off: the advisor answers with Quani own deterministic financial engine, and the photo of a receipt stays on your phone so you type the total in yourself. No model of any kind is downloaded.
  • In any case, if the model is not available, the built-in deterministic Advisor answers with the same calculations and the same figures.

Receipts are read with the on-device vision model when it is available. The image is never base64-encoded and never sent to any service: it is opened directly as a local file.

Network Transparency

3. Network Connections: Only One

Quani has no server of its own. This is the only connection the app can make, and they only happen when you choose to:

◆

Currency catalogue — api.frankfurter.dev

When you look up the list of currencies or a exchange rate, the app asks this public service for the available currencies or the rate of the day. The request does not include information about your accounts, your transactions, your photographs or any device identifier.

  • Dictation uses the iOS speech recognition engine. Depending on the language and the settings of your iPhone, the system may recognise speech on the device or rely on Apple's servers. In both cases what you dictate becomes an editable draft inside Quani, and the app does not forward it on its own.
  • Payment and commitment reminders are scheduled locally on the phone. There is no push notification server.
  • No financial data, photographs, advisor conversations, device identifiers or location data are sent to any service.
System Transparency

4. Use of iOS Permissions

  • Microphone and Speech Recognition: They activate only when you use manual dictation or double-tap the calendar to turn your voice into editable drafts of transactions or budgets.
  • Camera and Photo Library: They are used only when you choose to photograph a receipt or proof of payment, attach images to a transaction, or customise the icons of your categories and profiles.
  • Face ID: It is delegated entirely to the iOS operating system to unlock your local profile; Quani never accesses biometric data.
  • Local Notifications: They are scheduled on the device itself to remind you about payments and planned commitments.

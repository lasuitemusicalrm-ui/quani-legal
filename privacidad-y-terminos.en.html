01

Privacy Policy: your finances live on your iPhone

Quani was built on one strict principle: your wealth, your accounts and your financial habits belong to you alone. Quani has no servers of its own, asks for no email address or password and contains no advertising trackers.

Quani is designed to work offline: the accounting engine, the advisor, budgets and every calculation run inside the device. The few times the app or iOS connects to a service are described one by one in section 4, and none of them sends your accounts, transactions or budgets to Quani.

Local database per profileYour accounts (debit, credit, assets and liabilities), transactions, balances, budgets, folders, subscriptions and attached photos are stored in a SQLite database inside the phone, separate for each profile.
A profile name, nothing moreWhen you first open Quani you can type a name for your profile. It is only a local label: it does not create an account and is not sent to any server. A different name creates a new, independent profile.
PIN in the KeychainEach profile's PIN is kept encrypted in the native iOS Keychain and is never included in backups.
No analyticsQuani ships no analytics SDK, builds no advertising profile and does not measure how you use the app.
02

Backups and iCloud

Because there is no central server, backups are in your hands. Quani offers three paths:

  • Daily copy inside the app. Quani automatically keeps one copy per day (without photos) in its own space on the iPhone. If iCloud Backup is turned on for your iPhone, those copies travel with it.
  • Manual export. In More → Backup you can export a JSON file with your whole profile, photos included, or a CSV of your transactions, and keep it wherever you prefer.
  • iCloud Drive copy (optional). If you turn on “Save my profiles to iCloud” in More → Profiles, Quani stores your profiles in the “Quani” folder of your iCloud Drive so you can recover them when you install Quani on another iPhone (“Recover from iCloud”). Quani asks for confirmation before turning it on.
This data travels through your own iCloud account and is subject to Apple's terms; La Suite Musical has no access to it. The copy includes your data and photos, but never the PIN or security settings. When you delete a profile in Quani, its iCloud copy is deleted too.
03

Accounting engine and on-device intelligence

Queries, liquidity diagnoses, year-over-year comparisons and savings plans are processed inside your phone, without sending your statements to external services.

3.1 Advisor: deterministic engine

This is Quani's main engine. It works on every supported iPhone, offline too. It calculates your real balances, the debt accumulated after each transaction, calendar projections and financial priorities directly on your local database. No generative model can alter or invent accounting figures.

3.2 AsesorIA: depends on your iPhone

AsesorIA is an optional conversation layer. How it works depends on your phone:

  • iPhone 15 Pro or later, with iOS 26 or later and Apple Intelligence turned on: AsesorIA talks to the model built into iOS, which runs on your device and downloads no additional files. Figures always come from Quani's engine, not from the model.
  • Reading receipts with the camera also requires iOS 27 or later and the system offering its vision capability. The image is processed on the phone and is not sent to any service.
  • In any other case: the Advisor answers with the deterministic engine and the photo of a receipt stays on your phone so you can enter the total by hand. Quani downloads no language model.
04

Network connections and third parties

Quani has no server of its own. These are all the connections the app, or iOS on its behalf, can make, and when they happen:

Currency catalogue — api.frankfurter.dev

Only when you open the currency list

The app asks this public service for the list of currencies and the day's exchange rates. It sends only the code of your base currency (for example, MXN); as with any internet connection, the service sees the IP address. It includes no accounts, transactions, photos or device identifiers.

Voice dictation — Apple

Only when you dictate

It uses the iOS speech recognition engine. Depending on the language and your iPhone's settings, the system may recognise speech on the device or rely on Apple's servers. What you dictate becomes an editable draft inside Quani, and the app does not forward it.

iCloud Drive — Apple

Only if you turn on the iCloud copy

Your profiles travel to your iCloud Drive through Apple's services. Quani and La Suite Musical do not receive that data.

This website — GitHub Pages

Only when you open this page or the support page

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visits under its own policy. This site uses no cookies and no analytics.

  • No financial data, photographs, advisor conversations, device identifiers or location data are sent to any Quani service.
05

Use of iOS permissions

  • Microphone and Speech Recognition: they activate only when you use dictation or double-tap the calendar to turn your voice into editable drafts of transactions, budgets or advisor messages.
  • Camera and Photo Library: they are used only when you choose to photograph a receipt, attach images to a transaction, or set a photo for your profile or an icon.
  • Face ID: it is optional and off by default. iOS does the verification; Quani never accesses biometric data.
  • Notifications: local reminders for payments and commitments, scheduled by the phone itself. In each profile you choose how far ahead and at what time to be notified, or turn it off. If you allow it, the app also shows the number of pending items on its icon (badge). It is optional: you can deny the permission in iOS Settings and Quani keeps working.
  • iCloud: only if you turn on the iCloud Drive copy; your Apple account manages it in Settings.

Terms and conditions of use

6.1 Informative and organisational purpose

Quani, its budget charts, period statements and the suggestions of the Advisor and AsesorIA are personal organisation and information tools. In no case do they replace or represent certified financial, accounting, tax, investment or legal advice.

The Subscriptions catalogue and the names, colours or symbols of third-party services shown in it serve a strictly informative and organisational purpose, intended solely so you can identify and record on your own device the recurring expenses associated with those services. Quani does not sell, broker, manage or process payments for those services, and their inclusion implies no commercial affiliation, sponsorship or endorsement by their owners.

6.2 Your backups are your responsibility

Because no central server holds your information, Quani cannot recover data it does not have. Keep your own copies — exports or the iCloud copy — before you change, restore or erase your phone or the app.

6.3 Languages

Quani is available in 8 languages. Spanish is the reference language; the others are Beta versions under review by native speakers and may contain imprecise wording. If versions of this document differ, the Spanish version prevails.
